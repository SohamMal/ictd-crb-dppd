# Desirability — who benefits, and is it wanted?
**DPPD position:** Stage 1 › Desirability · **Status:** Thin; strengthen in final report · **Last updated:** 2026-10-06

## In plain words
The people who would use our results are NGOs and government agencies trying to reduce burning. A list of real, nearby places that already burn less, under the same conditions, is more useful to them than a general model. It tells them where to look for practices that work locally. We also need to be careful: naming low-burning places must not lead to blaming the high-burning ones.

## Technical summary
- **Target users:** NGOs working on CRM adoption; Punjab Agriculture Dept / PPCB; CRM scheme administrators.
- **Value proposition:** peer-group-specific PD examples ("tehsil X in group G burns less than its peers") support targeted replication instead of uniform interventions (A1 §3).
- **Ethics [P]:** tehsil-level aggregates only, no farmer-level identification; present negative deviants as places needing support, not enforcement targets; satellite detection errors acknowledged.
- **Gap [?]:** no stakeholder consultation recorded. Stage 3 fieldwork is likely desk-based only (`3.1/task_fieldwork_planning.md`). State this as a limitation.
